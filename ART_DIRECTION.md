# 美术与生成素材记录

前期保留明亮的儿童绘本官网；随现有章节逐渐加入不自然的动作、长时间注视、布偶缝线与旧档案影像。参考图用于理解古旧童话、手工人偶、仪式化构图和胶片质感，不复刻其构图或人物。

两张新图片均由内置 imagegen 生成，未使用 CLI。动画继续基于原创 SVG 的分层实现。

## 会员卡背面：小满的便条

仓库路径：`dist/assets/xiaoman-note.png`

页面用途：第 03 份证据的卡片背面；保留「辨认字迹」文字版本与图片替代文本。

生成提示词：

> Generate a single authentic physical handwritten note artifact for a Chinese mystery game. Landscape photograph scan, about 3:2 aspect ratio, the reverse of a small cream-yellow children's membership postcard. The entire composition is the paper itself nearly edge-to-edge with softly worn corners, slight pencil smudges, a faint fold, warm natural light, very subtle shadow around its outer edge on a plain off-white background. It was handwritten by an approximately eight-year-old Chinese child using a real dark graphite pencil. Handwriting should be naive, uneven in size and baseline, a little hesitant with pressure variations and slightly wobbly strokes; NOT elegant adult calligraphy, NOT a typeset font, NOT bubble lettering. Nevertheless all Chinese characters must be correct and clearly legible. The handwriting is large and occupies most of the note. Exact handwritten text only, in four loose lines:
>
> 去看朋友们的一天。
>
> 最后一段晚安，
>
> 不要快进。
>
> 小满
>
> The final signature 小满 is smaller, near the lower right. Below and beside the handwriting, a tiny simple child's pencil doodle of a long-eared smiling rabbit with a very small pale green crayon waistcoat, and one small yellow crayon flower in a corner. Naive primitive doodles, small enough not to compete with the written clue. Overall affectionate, private, fragile, not overt horror. No blood, no weapons, no medical content. No printed headings, no extra text, no numbers, no UI, no watermark. Make this look like an actual child wrote an urgent little reminder on the back of their card, not like a designed stationery product. This asset will be displayed 480 px wide in a website modal, so keep the requested text large and readable.

## 母带附件：布景之外的布布

仓库路径：`dist/assets/archive-mascot.png`

页面用途：第 05 份证据的原始影像附件，第三章后可查看；观察兔子人偶、圈状摆放的道具与旧绘本。

生成提示词：

> Create one original landscape 3:2 archival analog photograph for a fictional mystery exploration game called Bubu's Happy Garden. It will be an unsettling found production still that is discovered in the later part of an investigation, not a jump scare. Scene: an abandoned vintage children's television studio backstage corridor with dingy pale olive ceramic wall tiles, old wooden doors and muted institutional fluorescent light. On the right half, one life-size handmade white long-eared rabbit mascot puppet in a faded green waistcoat leans around a doorway, head tilted gently sideways, huge hollow dark glass eyes, subtle sewn closed smile, aged linen and papier-mache fur texture. Its face is fully visible and readable but quietly uncanny, no fangs, no monstrous transformation. In the shadowy far background on the left, a small blurred circle of cream rabbit marionettes sits on the floor like forgotten props, creating the suggestion of a strange folk-tale ritual without explicit religious symbols. No living children or other humans. A very old illustrated storybook lies on a wooden crate in the foreground, its rabbit engravings merely barely visible, hints of medieval manuscript border texture. Mood combines antique European illustrated folk tales, handcrafted stop-motion puppetry, a liminal empty public building, and a found 1990s color film print. Muted moss green, amber, tobacco sepia and faded ivory, a little dusty rose inside the ears; tactile mottled paper print, subtle fine film grain, soft lens, uneven vignette; not black and white, not cyberpunk, not glossy digital 3D. Strong photographic composition, subject about 45 percent of the image, adequate empty corridor space on the left; enough light to distinguish the puppet clearly at mobile size. Edge-to-edge image, no white border, no collage panels, no text, no watermark, no logos. No weapons, no violence, no blood, no gore. Original character and original composition; do not reproduce any existing movie frame or artwork.
